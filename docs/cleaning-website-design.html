Questions people ask before booking

How much does house cleaning cost in Melbourne?

A regular clean of a 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom home is $251 one-off or $213 fortnightly. The price tool at the top of this page shows your exact price.

Do you guarantee my bond back?

Yes. If your agent flags anything on our end of lease checklist within 72 hours, we come back and re-clean it free.

Do I need to be home?

No. Most customers leave a key or a lockbox code. Every cleaner is police-checked and insured.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes, including a vacuum and eco-friendly products. Tell us if you'd like us to use yours.

Will I get the same cleaner each time?

For regular cleans, yes. If your cleaner is away, we text you who's coming instead.

How do I cancel or reschedule?

Reply to your booking text. It's free up to 24 hours before your clean.
